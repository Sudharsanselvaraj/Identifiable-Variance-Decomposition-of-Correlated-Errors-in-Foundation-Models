## Status legend

- **Killed** — permanently rejected (may stay as background/rationale).
- **Alive** — the claim/experiment stands.
- **Armed** — pre-registered condition under which it dies; not yet triggered.

## Review rule

A submission is not ready until every claim in it has a Kill-Test entry, and every
"armed" condition is honestly reported if triggered.
